Have you ever wondered how plants eat without a mouth? They actually make their own meals, and the process is called **photosynthesis**. 

Think of a green leaf as a **tiny solar-powered kitchen**, and the plant as a master chef!

To cook its favorite meal, the plant chef needs three simple ingredients:
1. **Water** (drawn up through the straw-like roots in the dirt)
2. **Air** (caught by tiny invisible pores on the leaves)
3. **Sunlight** (the "stove" that provides the cooking power)

The plant mixes the water and air using the power of the sun. Ding! The meal is ready. The plant gets **sweet plant food** to help it grow big and strong. 

As a wonderful bonus, the kitchen releases fresh **oxygen** into the air—which is the exact air you and I need to breathe!

---

### A Quick Memory Trick
The word sounds giant, but break it in half:
* **Photo** = *Light* (like a bright camera flash!)
* **Synthesis** = *Putting things together*

So, **Photosynthesis** just means: **"Putting a meal together using light!"**
